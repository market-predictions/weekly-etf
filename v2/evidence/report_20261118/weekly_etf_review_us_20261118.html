How this portfolio works

  1. Core: SPY (US equities) and AGG (US bonds). Around it 5 satellites of about 8% and a small cash position (target 3%).
  2. Every week all funds are ranked on 3-, 6- and 12-month returns in euro; the strongest in an uptrend fill the satellite slots, one per theme, sector or region.
  3. A satellite stays while it ranks in the top 15 and its trend is up; a newcomer replaces it only if it scores at least 25 points higher. A trend sale follows two weeks below the 200-day average.
  4. Defensive when US equities are in a downtrend and breadth is weak: 3 satellites, the rest in short-dated US Treasury bills (BIL).
  5. At most 2 fund changes per week. Aim: keep up with the market and limit large losses; the rules do not promise to beat the benchmark.
  6. The European mirror follows every decision in UCITS funds at the next European close; a line is only resized when it is more than 2 points off.

Data and method

Benchmark: 70% US equities (SPY) and 30% US bonds (AGG), in euro, rebalanced weekly; for the mirror 70% SXR8 and 30% EUNA. Prices: closing prices of the US funds in dollars (Yahoo Finance), holdings checked against Alpha Vantage, converted to euro at the same day's EUR/USD close; the mirror at euro closing prices on European exchanges. Returns are price returns without dividends. Trades are modelled in whole shares with an estimated 0.10% cost. The return since inception (28 March 2026) links the previous report system's value up to 7 August to the recalculated value after it. The commentary is drafted with the help of AI from the figures in this report only; every number in the text is checked automatically.
