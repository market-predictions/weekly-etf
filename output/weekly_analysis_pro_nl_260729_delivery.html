  • Status: Verhoogd maar gelokaliseerd.

Wat veranderde

Portefeuille-implicaties

  • Stay invested, maar make new capital pass a stricter macro and relative-strength filter.
  • Treat implementation-sensitive and weak-contribution positions as active reviews rather than passive holds.
  • Do not fund replacement candidates until the pricing basis and direct duel evidence are visible.
